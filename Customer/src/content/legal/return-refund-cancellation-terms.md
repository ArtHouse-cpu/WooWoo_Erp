## 3. GENERAL POLICY PRINCIPLE

Returns, exchanges, cancellations, and refunds are not automatic merely because a customer changes their mind.

Eligibility depends on the transaction category, Membership benefits, timing, product or Service status, condition of goods, preparation or performance already completed, and applicable law.

Where this Policy states that an item, Membership, booking, order, or Service is non-refundable, that restriction applies to voluntary or change-of-mind refund requests to the maximum extent permitted by law.

Mandatory consumer remedies remain unaffected.

## 4. PROOF OF PURCHASE

WOOWOO may require reasonable proof of purchase before processing a return, exchange, cancellation, or refund.

Proof may include:

- a WOOWOO account transaction;
- invoice or receipt;
- order number;
- registered mobile number;
- payment reference;
- Membership record; or
- another reasonably verifiable transaction record.

WOOWOO may decline a discretionary return or exchange where the transaction cannot reasonably be verified.

This requirement will not be applied arbitrarily to defeat a genuine statutory claim where the purchase can otherwise be reasonably established.

## 5. STORE PRODUCT RETURN WINDOW

Eligibility for return or exchange of Store products is based on the applicable Membership benefits and transaction conditions.

Where a customer's active Membership benefit expressly provides product return or exchange access, an eligible product may be presented for return or exchange within one (1) calendar day of purchase, unless a different benefit is expressly communicated for the relevant Membership plan or transaction.

For the purpose of the standard one-day window, the product must be presented no later than the end of the calendar day immediately following the purchase date, subject to Store operating hours.

Customers without an applicable return or exchange benefit are not entitled to a discretionary change-of-mind return or exchange merely because another Membership tier includes such a benefit.

Nothing in this Section restricts a mandatory remedy available under applicable law for defective, materially misdescribed, unsafe, counterfeit, or otherwise legally non-conforming goods.

## 6. PRODUCT CONDITION REQUIREMENT

A Store product presented for discretionary return or exchange must be in its original condition.

Original condition ordinarily means that the product:

- is unused;
- is not materially opened where opening affects resale, hygiene, safety, or product integrity;
- is not damaged after purchase;
- has not been altered, cut, mixed, sharpened, consumed, filled, assembled, or customised;
- includes original packaging where applicable;
- includes tags, labels, accessories, parts, manuals, or bundled items supplied with it; and
- remains reasonably capable of being returned to sale as a new product, where relevant.

The fact that a product is physically present within the one-day window does not by itself establish eligibility.

The product remains subject to physical inspection by an authorised WOOWOO representative.

## 7. PHYSICAL INSPECTION AND FINAL ELIGIBILITY DECISION

All discretionary Store product returns and exchanges are subject to physical inspection by an authorised WOOWOO representative.

The representative may reasonably inspect:

- signs of use;
- packaging;
- seals;
- quantity;
- product surface;
- accessories;
- labels;
- damage;
- contamination;
- alteration;
- missing components; and
- other characteristics relevant to original condition.

The final internal decision on eligibility for a discretionary return or exchange will be based on the physical inspection and applicable Membership benefit.

WOOWOO may reject a discretionary return or exchange where the product is not in original condition or the applicable benefit does not provide return or exchange eligibility.

This internal eligibility decision does not eliminate a customer's right to raise a genuine defect, deficiency, misdescription, or other mandatory consumer claim under applicable law.

## 8. EXCHANGE BEFORE REFUND FOR CHANGE-OF-MIND RETURNS

Where a discretionary Store return is accepted under a Membership benefit, WOOWOO may provide the remedy specified by that benefit.

Depending on the applicable benefit, WOOWOO may permit:

- exchange for the same product;
- exchange for another eligible product;
- Store credit;
- WOOWOO Wallet credit; or
- another expressly stated benefit.

A discretionary change-of-mind return does not automatically create a right to a cash or original-payment-method refund unless the applicable Membership benefit or transaction terms expressly provide one.

Where law requires a refund or another specific remedy, the mandatory remedy will apply.

## 9. NON-RETURNABLE PRODUCT CATEGORIES

Unless a mandatory legal remedy applies, discretionary returns or exchanges may be refused for:

- used art materials;
- opened paints, inks, mediums, chemicals, adhesives, or liquids;
- cut paper, canvas, board, fabric, or material sold to customer specifications;
- customised or personalised products;
- mixed or specially prepared colours or materials;
- opened hygiene-sensitive goods;
- consumable products;
- clearance or final-sale products expressly identified as such before purchase;
- gift cards or equivalent stored-value instruments, subject to applicable terms;
- damaged products where the damage occurred after handover;
- incomplete sets or products with missing components; and
- products otherwise excluded under the applicable Membership benefit.

A product will not be treated as non-returnable merely to avoid a mandatory remedy for a genuine defect or legal non-conformity.

## 10. DEFECTIVE, DAMAGED OR INCORRECT PRODUCTS

If a product is defective, materially damaged before handover, materially misdescribed, incorrect, counterfeit, unsafe, or otherwise legally non-conforming, the customer should notify WOOWOO promptly.

WOOWOO may reasonably inspect the product and transaction.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, an appropriate remedy may include:

- replacement;
- exchange;
- repair where appropriate;
- correction;
- proportionate refund;
- full refund; or
- another legally required remedy.

The one-day discretionary return window does not extinguish a statutory remedy that lawfully applies to a genuine product defect or other actionable non-conformity.

## 11. DAMAGE AFTER PURCHASE

WOOWOO is not responsible for damage caused solely after handover by misuse, improper storage, accident, unauthorised alteration, contamination, neglect, or use contrary to clear product instructions.

Normal wear and tear is not a product defect.

Art and stationery products may react differently depending on paper, surface, climate, storage, technique, or other materials.

A subjective dissatisfaction with artistic output does not by itself establish a product defect where the product is genuine, correctly supplied, and performs within its represented characteristics.

## 12. MEMBERSHIP — NO REFUND POLICY

WOOWOO Membership fees are non-refundable after successful activation.

Once a Membership is activated on the Member's account, no refund will be provided for:

- change of mind;
- non-use or limited use of Membership;
- failure to visit WOOWOO;
- failure to redeem available benefits;
- relocation;
- personal schedule changes;
- purchase of another Membership;
- dissatisfaction arising solely from an expectation not included in the Membership Terms; or
- a voluntary request to cancel Membership.

This no-refund policy applies to General Membership, lifetime Memberships, paid Membership upgrades, and other Membership plans unless a specific written offer expressly states otherwise.

Where a transaction was duplicated, unauthorised, charged despite failed activation, materially misrepresented, or is otherwise subject to a mandatory refund or remedy under applicable law, WOOWOO will review and provide the legally required or appropriate remedy.

Nothing in this Section permits WOOWOO to retain payment for a Membership that WOOWOO never activated or materially failed to provide where a refund or other remedy is required by law.

## 13. MEMBERSHIP CANCELLATION ON DEMAND

A Member may request cancellation of Membership on demand through an authorised WOOWOO support channel.

Cancellation means termination or deactivation of future Membership access and benefits, subject to technical processing and verification.

Membership cancellation is voluntary and does not create a refund entitlement for Membership fees already paid or an activated Membership.

On cancellation:

- future access to Membership benefits may cease;
- unused Membership privileges may lapse;
- Membership-specific pricing or priority access may cease;
- future cashback eligibility linked solely to active Membership may cease; and
- the account may continue to exist as a non-Member account unless separately deleted or otherwise restricted.

Accrued Wallet balances, valid completed transactions, statutory rights, and obligations that survive cancellation will be handled under the applicable terms.

A cancellation request should be made by the Member or an authorised person and may require reasonable account verification.

## 14. MEMBERSHIP UPGRADE PAYMENTS

A successfully activated Membership upgrade is non-refundable on the same basis as an activated Membership.

Where an upgrade payment is successful but the purchased upgrade is not activated, the Member should contact WOOWOO for correction.

WOOWOO may activate the correct plan, correct the Membership record, reverse a duplicate charge, or provide another appropriate remedy.

A Member cannot obtain a refund merely because the Member later decides to return to a lower plan after the upgraded benefits have been activated.

## 15. MEMBERSHIP CASHBACK AND WELCOME BENEFITS

Cashback, welcome credits, Wallet benefits, gifts, or promotional privileges associated with Membership may be subject to separate Wallet or offer conditions.

Where a Membership transaction is lawfully reversed or refunded, benefits issued solely because of that transaction may be reversed.

A Member cancelling an activated non-refundable Membership does not become entitled to convert unused Membership benefits into cash.

Fraudulent creation of multiple accounts to repeatedly claim welcome benefits is prohibited.

## 16. SPACE BOOKING CANCELLATION

A Space Booker may request cancellation through an authorised WOOWOO channel.

The applicable cancellation outcome may depend on the booking terms communicated before confirmation, the time remaining before the booking, reserved capacity, setup, external commitments, and whether the booking has commenced.

Once a Space booking has commenced, change-of-mind cancellation may be non-refundable.

A no-show may be non-refundable.

Where WOOWOO cancels a paid booking and cannot provide the booked Space or an agreed alternative, the Booker may be entitled to an appropriate refund or remedy.

The WOOWOO Space Terms also apply.

## 17. WOOFOO ORDER CANCELLATION

A WOOFOO order may be cancelled on request before preparation begins, subject to confirmation by WOOFOO.

Once food or beverages enter preparation, a change-of-mind cancellation may be refused because the order is prepared for the customer and may be perishable.

Where WOOFOO cannot supply a prepaid item and the customer does not accept an agreed substitute, an eligible refund will be processed.

Incorrect, missing, unsafe, spoiled, or materially deficient food complaints will be handled according to the WOOFOO Café Terms and applicable law.

Participant cancellation is subject to the Event-specific cancellation conditions communicated before registration or purchase.

## 19. EVENT CANCELLATION OR RESCHEDULING BY WOOWOO

Where WOOWOO cancels a paid Event and does not provide the contracted Event, eligible participants may be offered a transfer, agreed alternative, voluntarily accepted credit, or eligible monetary refund.

WOOWOO will not force Wallet credit instead of a monetary refund where a monetary refund is legally or contractually required.

## 20. SERVICES CANCELLATION

A customer may request cancellation of a WOOWOO Service.

Where work has not commenced and no customer-specific cost has been incurred, the cancellation outcome will be determined according to the applicable Service terms.

Where work, consultation, design, procurement, production, vendor commitment, or other performance has commenced, WOOWOO may account for:

- work already performed;
- approved milestones;
- non-recoverable materials;
- customer-specific procurement;
- external vendor commitments; and
- other direct project costs to the extent permitted by law.

Custom, personalised, or substantially commenced Services may be non-refundable for change of mind.

The WOOWOO Services Terms also apply.
