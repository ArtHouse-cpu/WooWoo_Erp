## 1. OUR COMMUNITY

Welcome to WOOWOO ART HOUSE® — House of Creatives.

WOOWOO ART HOUSE® ("WOOWOO", "we", "us", or "our") is a registered trademark and a venture owned and operated by JHA SONS AND ARRAY LLP.

WOOWOO was created as a place where creators, students, artists, designers, learners, professionals, families, businesses, and curious people can discover, create, work, learn, meet, and grow.

These Community Guidelines ("Guidelines") explain the standards of behaviour expected across WOOWOO premises, Stores, Spaces, WOOFOO café areas, Events, Services, memberships, digital experiences, community groups, and other officially operated WOOWOO environments.

Our aim is simple: creativity should feel welcoming, respectful, safe, and human.

By participating in the WOOWOO community, you agree to follow these Guidelines together with the applicable WOOWOO terms and policies.

## 2. WHO THESE GUIDELINES APPLY TO

These Guidelines apply to:

- Members;
- customers;
- visitors;
- creators and artists;
- students and learners;
- parents and guardians;
- Event participants;
- workshop attendees;
- Space Bookers and guests;
- exhibitors and vendors;
- speakers and facilitators;
- partners and collaborators;
- Service customers; and
- users of official WOOWOO digital or community channels.

A person does not need to hold a paid Membership for these Guidelines to apply while participating in a WOOWOO environment.

## 3. HOUSE OF CREATIVES PRINCIPLE

WOOWOO is built around creative participation and community.

We expect users to contribute to an environment where people can:

- explore without unnecessary intimidation;
- create without harassment;
- learn without humiliation;
- share ideas without being personally attacked;
- meet others with reasonable confidence in their safety; and
- enjoy WOOWOO facilities responsibly.

These principles do not require everyone to agree with each other.

Creative critique, disagreement, debate, and differing artistic views are permitted. The line is crossed when disagreement becomes harassment, threats, targeted abuse, unlawful discrimination, or serious disruption.

## 4. RESPECT PEOPLE

Treat other users, staff, creators, facilitators, and visitors with reasonable respect.

Do not:

- threaten another person;
- repeatedly insult or humiliate a targeted individual;
- intimidate someone to force participation or silence;
- engage in aggressive confrontation that creates a genuine safety concern;
- deliberately interfere with another person's lawful use of WOOWOO; or
- encourage others to target or harass an individual.

A single disagreement or ordinary criticism is not automatically harassment. Context, severity, repetition, and impact may be considered.

## 5. HARASSMENT AND BULLYING

Harassment and bullying are prohibited.

This includes serious or repeated:

- unwanted targeting;
- stalking behaviour;
- threats;
- intimidation;
- sexual harassment;
- humiliating conduct;
- malicious sharing of private information;
- coercive behaviour; or
- conduct intended to make another person reasonably fear for their safety.

WOOWOO may intervene even where the conduct occurs through an official community group or digital channel rather than physically on the premises.

Genuine reports of harassment will be assessed according to available information and circumstances.

## 6. DISCRIMINATORY ABUSE

WOOWOO does not permit targeted discriminatory abuse or unlawful discriminatory conduct.

Users must not direct degrading, threatening, or abusive conduct at a person because of a protected characteristic under applicable law.

This Guideline does not prohibit respectful cultural discussion, creative exploration, academic discussion, or good-faith debate.

WOOWOO may consider context, artistic purpose, educational value, severity, and applicable law when assessing creative or expressive content.

## 7. SEXUAL HARASSMENT AND UNWANTED CONDUCT

Sexual harassment, unwanted sexual advances, sexual intimidation, and non-consensual sexual conduct are prohibited.

Do not:

- repeatedly pursue someone after a clear refusal;
- make unwanted sexual contact;
- pressure another person for sexual interaction;
- secretly record a person in a private context;
- use WOOWOO community access to stalk another person; or
- threaten retaliation after rejection.

Where conduct may constitute a criminal offence or immediate safety risk, WOOWOO may contact or cooperate with appropriate authorities.

## 8. VIOLENCE AND THREATS

Violence, credible threats of violence, and conduct creating an immediate serious safety risk are prohibited.

Users must not:

- physically attack another person;
- threaten serious physical harm;
- intentionally use tools or materials as weapons;
- encourage imminent violence; or
- interfere with emergency response.

WOOWOO may immediately remove a person, stop an Event or booking, secure an area, or contact emergency or law enforcement authorities where reasonably necessary.

## 9. WEAPONS AND DANGEROUS ITEMS

Weapons and unlawful dangerous items are prohibited.

Creative tools such as cutters, blades, sculpting tools, heat tools, or similar equipment may be permitted only for legitimate creative or operational use.

A permitted creative tool must not be used to threaten, intimidate, or harm another person.

WOOWOO may restrict or remove an item where its possession or use creates a material safety risk.

## 10. CHILDREN AND YOUNG CREATORS

WOOWOO welcomes young creators subject to applicable age, supervision, and activity requirements.

Adults must behave appropriately around minors.

Users must not:

- sexually exploit or groom a minor;
- solicit sexual content from a minor;
- intentionally expose a minor to unlawful sexual material;
- use WOOWOO to facilitate abuse of a child; or
- knowingly endanger a minor.

Parents, guardians, schools, organisers, or responsible adults remain responsible for supervision where the applicable Event, Space, or Service arrangement requires it.

Suspected child safety concerns may be escalated in accordance with applicable law.

## 11. CONSENT AND PERSONAL BOUNDARIES

Respect reasonable personal boundaries.

A person participating in the community does not automatically consent to:

- physical contact;
- close-up photography;
- private messaging of a sexual nature;
- romantic attention;
- use of their work;
- sharing of their personal contact information; or
- participation in another person's content.

Where consent is relevant, ask clearly and respect a refusal.

Consent may be withdrawn for future conduct, subject to lawful and already completed contractual uses.

## 12. PRIVACY AND PERSONAL INFORMATION

Do not misuse another person's personal information.

Users must not maliciously publish or distribute another person's private phone number, home address, financial information, identification documents, private communications, or other sensitive information without lawful authority.

Do not obtain another person's OTP, password, UPI PIN, card PIN, or banking credentials.

Official WOOWOO staff will not ask for a UPI PIN, card PIN, or banking password.

Privacy matters are also governed by the WOOWOO ART HOUSE® Privacy Policy.

## 13. PHOTOGRAPHY AND RECORDING

Photography and recording may be permitted in many WOOWOO environments, subject to Event, Space, privacy, and safety rules.

Users must not use photography or recording to harass, stalk, exploit, or unlawfully invade another person's privacy.

Professional production, commercial filming, large equipment, or recording of restricted activities may require prior approval.

Where an Event or area restricts recording, users must comply with the communicated restriction.

Reasonable privacy concerns should be raised with WOOWOO staff.

## 14. RESPECT CREATIVE WORK

Creators retain rights in their original work subject to applicable law and any specific agreement.

Do not:

- steal artwork;
- falsely claim authorship;
- intentionally remove creator attribution where attribution is legally or contractually required;
- reproduce protected work unlawfully;
- sell counterfeit creative products; or
- use another creator's work in a misleading manner.

Being inspired by a style, movement, technique, or public creative trend does not automatically establish infringement.

Intellectual property concerns will be assessed according to applicable law and the relevant facts.

## 15. PLAGIARISM AND FALSE AUTHORSHIP

Users must not knowingly present another person's original work as their own.

Competitions, exhibitions, showcases, and applications may have stricter originality rules.

WOOWOO may request reasonable information concerning authorship where a credible concern arises.

A similarity allegation alone does not automatically establish plagiarism.

Where material fraud or plagiarism is established, WOOWOO may remove the submission, reverse an improperly obtained benefit, or apply Event-specific consequences.

## 16. CREATIVE CRITIQUE

Thoughtful critique is welcome.

Critique the work, idea, technique, presentation, or process without turning criticism into targeted abuse.

Examples of acceptable engagement may include:

- explaining why a composition feels unbalanced;
- discussing technical weaknesses;
- disagreeing with an artistic decision; or
- suggesting another approach.

Repeatedly humiliating a creator, threatening them, or organising targeted abuse is not acceptable critique.

## 17. SAFE USE OF ART MATERIALS

Use creative materials, tools, and equipment responsibly.

Follow reasonable instructions concerning:

- cutting tools;
- heat tools;
- electrical equipment;
- solvents;
- aerosols;
- resin;
- pigments;
- adhesives;
- ventilation; and
- protective equipment.

Do not pour prohibited materials into sinks or drains.

Do not bring highly hazardous, illegal, or materially unsafe substances into WOOWOO without express approval and lawful handling arrangements.

Report significant spills, equipment faults, or safety concerns promptly.

## 18. RESPECT SHARED SPACES

WOOWOO Spaces and common areas are shared creative environments.

Users must:

- use the area for the approved purpose;
- respect booking times;
- avoid unauthorised occupation of another booked Space;
- keep emergency routes clear;
- use furniture and equipment carefully;
- manage noise reasonably; and
- leave the Space in an appropriate condition.

Specific Space use is also governed by the WOOWOO Space Terms.

## 19. CLEANLINESS AND WASTE

Help keep the House of Creatives usable for everyone.

Dispose of ordinary waste in designated areas.

Do not intentionally leave excessive waste, food, paint, clay, chemicals, or other materials in shared areas.

Follow instructions for specialist material disposal.

Where a user's activity causes excessive or specialist cleaning beyond ordinary use, reasonable charges may apply under the relevant Terms.

## 20. WOOFOO CAFÉ CONDUCT

Users of WOOFOO café areas must treat staff and other customers respectfully.

Do not enter food preparation or staff-only areas without permission.

Do not intentionally contaminate food, utensils, or service areas.

Food safety concerns should be reported promptly and genuinely.

False contamination claims or fabricated refund requests are prohibited.

WOOFOO use is also governed by the WOOFOO Café Terms.

## 21. EVENT CONDUCT

Participants must follow reasonable Event, facilitator, entry, capacity, and safety instructions.

Do not intentionally disrupt an Event so that others cannot reasonably participate.

Do not counterfeit tickets, manipulate eligibility, or fraudulently obtain Event benefits.

Creative disagreement with a speaker, artist, or participant does not justify threats or harassment.

Event participation is also governed by the WOOWOO Events Terms.

## 22. STORE CONDUCT

Customers must not steal, intentionally damage products, tamper with packaging for fraudulent purposes, switch price labels, or submit fabricated return claims.

Products and testers must be used according to Store instructions.

Children should be appropriately supervised around fragile, sharp, chemical, or specialist art products.

Store purchases are also governed by the WOOWOO Store Terms.

## 23. SERVICES CONDUCT

Customers engaging WOOWOO Services must provide reasonably accurate information and must not request unlawful, fraudulent, counterfeit, or intentionally infringing work.

Do not threaten or harass staff, creators, or vendors in an attempt to obtain unauthorised refunds, free work, or scope outside an agreed Service.

Genuine complaints and reasonable negotiation are welcome.

Services are also governed by the WOOWOO Services Terms.

## 24. DIGITAL AND COMMUNITY CHANNELS

These Guidelines may apply to official WOOWOO digital communities, messaging groups, community features, comment areas, or other channels operated by WOOWOO.

Users must not use official channels for:

- targeted harassment;
- malicious doxxing;
- fraudulent impersonation;
- scams;
- phishing;
- repeated unsolicited commercial spam;
- unlawful threats; or
- distribution of malicious links intended to steal credentials.

WOOWOO may moderate official community channels according to these Guidelines and applicable law.

## 25. SPAM AND UNSOLICITED PROMOTION

Reasonable creative networking and sharing may be permitted.

Users must not repeatedly send unwanted promotional messages, mass solicit members through scraped contact information, or use official community channels primarily for deceptive lead generation.

Event or group-specific promotional rules may apply.

WOOWOO may remove repeated spam or restrict the relevant posting or messaging privilege.

## 26. FRAUD, SCAMS AND DECEPTION

Fraud and scams are prohibited.

Do not:

- impersonate WOOWOO staff;
- create fake payment confirmations;
- submit false refund claims;
- manipulate cashback;
- misuse coupons through fraudulent accounts;
- sell fake tickets;
- fabricate competition engagement;
- solicit banking credentials; or
- deceive another user for unlawful financial gain.

Suspected fraud may be investigated and relevant records preserved in accordance with law.

## 27. IMPERSONATION

Do not falsely represent yourself as:

- WOOWOO staff;
- an official WOOWOO partner;
- another creator;
- a business you do not represent;
- a facilitator;
- a judge; or
- another identifiable person or organisation where the impersonation is deceptive or harmful.

Parody, artistic performance, or clearly fictional expression is not automatically prohibited where it is not used to materially deceive others.

## 28. MISUSE OF MEMBERSHIP BENEFITS

Membership privileges are subject to the applicable Membership Terms.

Users must not:

- fraudulently share a personal benefit;
- create duplicate accounts to obtain repeated welcome benefits;
- manipulate cashback;
- resell complimentary access without permission;
- falsely claim Member status; or
- exploit a technical error to obtain benefits they know they are not entitled to.

WOOWOO may correct proven benefit errors and reverse fraudulently obtained benefits.

Good-faith use of an unclear benefit will not automatically be treated as fraud.

## 29. PROHIBITED COMMERCIAL ACTIVITY

A visit, Membership, café purchase, or Space booking does not automatically authorise commercial sales or solicitation.

Stalls, pop-ups, paid workshops, commercial filming, ticketed activities, or product sales may require approval or a specific arrangement.

Users must not mislead customers into believing an independent business is operated, endorsed, or guaranteed by WOOWOO.

Approved commercial participants remain responsible for their independent legal and consumer obligations.

## 30. ALCOHOL, SMOKING AND PROHIBITED SUBSTANCES

Smoking and vaping are prohibited except in a lawful area expressly designated for such activity, if any.

Alcohol must not be brought, sold, served, or consumed without express approval and compliance with applicable law.

Illegal drugs and unlawful substances are prohibited.

A person whose intoxicated conduct creates a material safety risk or serious disruption may be refused service or required to leave.

## 31. PROPERTY AND THEFT

Do not steal, intentionally damage, vandalise, or unlawfully remove property belonging to WOOWOO or another person.

Users should take reasonable care of personal belongings.

Found items should be handed to WOOWOO staff where practicable.

WOOWOO may investigate credible theft or property damage concerns and may cooperate with appropriate authorities.

## 32. EMERGENCIES

In an emergency, follow reasonable instructions from WOOWOO staff, emergency personnel, or competent authorities.

Do not block emergency exits, tamper with extinguishers, interfere with evacuation, or create a false emergency.

Where there is an immediate threat to life or safety, users should contact appropriate emergency services.

WOOWOO may evacuate, close, or restrict an area where reasonably necessary.

## 33. REPORTING A CONCERN

Users may report a Community Guidelines concern to WOOWOO staff or through official contact channels.

A useful report may include:

- what happened;
- when and where it happened;
- the people involved, where known;
- relevant booking or Event details; and
- supporting information reasonably available.

Users are not required to personally confront someone before making a report.

For an immediate emergency, contact appropriate emergency services rather than relying solely on a routine WOOWOO support channel.

## 34. GOOD-FAITH REPORTS

WOOWOO encourages genuine safety and conduct reports.

A person will not be treated as having violated these Guidelines merely because a good-faith report is not ultimately substantiated.

Knowingly fabricated allegations, deliberately falsified evidence, or malicious reporting intended to defraud or seriously harm another person may itself violate these Guidelines.

A disagreement about facts does not automatically establish malicious reporting.

## 35. HOW WOOWOO MAY REVIEW REPORTS

WOOWOO may review relevant information reasonably available to it.

Depending on the matter, this may include:

- statements from involved persons;
- staff observations;
- booking or transaction information;
- CCTV where lawfully available;
- Event records;
- official community messages; or
- other relevant information.

WOOWOO is not a court or law enforcement body.

Internal action under these Guidelines is a contractual and operational decision concerning WOOWOO environments.

Criminal or statutory matters may be referred to competent authorities.

## 36. INTERIM SAFETY MEASURES

While a serious concern is reviewed, WOOWOO may take temporary and proportionate measures where reasonably necessary.

Measures may include:

- separating individuals;
- changing a booking arrangement;
- restricting access to a specific Event or Space;
- pausing an account benefit;
- requiring communication through a designated channel; or
- temporarily restricting premises access.

An interim measure does not itself establish wrongdoing.

WOOWOO may revise the measure as additional information becomes available.

## 37. ENFORCEMENT ACTIONS

Depending on seriousness, repetition, risk, and context, WOOWOO may:

- provide guidance or a reminder;
- issue a warning;
- remove content from an official channel;
- stop an activity;
- require a person to leave an Event or Space;
- cancel a booking;
- restrict a specific feature or benefit;
- suspend Membership privileges;
- temporarily restrict premises access;
- terminate Membership according to applicable Membership Terms;
- permanently restrict access in serious cases; or
- contact appropriate authorities.

WOOWOO may act immediately in a serious safety situation.

Enforcement should be proportionate to the circumstances and subject to applicable law.

## 38. SERIOUS VIOLATIONS

Conduct that may justify immediate or significant action includes:

- serious violence;
- credible threats;
- sexual assault or serious sexual harassment;
- child exploitation;
- theft;
- deliberate major property damage;
- serious fraud;
- weapons misuse;
- intentional dangerous contamination;
- stalking;
- repeated severe harassment; or
- another material and immediate safety or legal risk.

This list is illustrative and not exhaustive.

## 39. REPEATED VIOLATIONS

Repeated lower-level violations may result in stronger action where prior guidance or warnings have not resolved the behaviour.

WOOWOO may consider the nature of earlier incidents, time elapsed, similarity, severity, and subsequent conduct.

A historical minor issue will not automatically justify permanent exclusion.

Patterns of deliberate abuse may be treated more seriously.

## 40. MEMBERSHIP AND COMMUNITY ACCESS

Membership is a privilege governed by the Membership Terms.

A Community Guidelines violation may affect Membership benefits or access where the conduct materially relates to WOOWOO Services, premises, users, or safety.

Any Membership suspension or termination will be handled according to the Membership Terms, these Guidelines, and applicable law.

Termination of community access does not automatically eliminate an accrued monetary refund right that exists under mandatory law.

## 41. REMOVAL FROM PREMISES

WOOWOO may require a person to leave its premises where reasonably necessary to protect safety, prevent serious disruption, respond to unlawful conduct, or enforce a material rule.

A person directed to leave must do so peacefully.

Refusal to leave after lawful withdrawal of permission may result in contact with appropriate authorities.

Removal from one Event or Space does not automatically mean permanent exclusion unless WOOWOO communicates a broader restriction.

## 42. RESTRICTION OF FUTURE ACCESS

In serious or repeated cases, WOOWOO may restrict future access to selected or all WOOWOO premises, Events, Spaces, or community channels.

The scope and duration of a restriction may depend on safety risk, seriousness, repetition, and available information.

WOOWOO may review a restriction where material new information becomes available.

Nothing in these Guidelines prevents WOOWOO from taking urgent lawful action to protect persons or property.

## 43. COOPERATION WITH AUTHORITIES

WOOWOO may preserve information and cooperate with law enforcement, courts, regulators, emergency services, child protection authorities, or other competent bodies where required or permitted by law.

WOOWOO may make a report where it reasonably believes a serious criminal, safety, or child protection concern requires escalation.

Personal data will be handled according to the Privacy Policy and applicable law.

## 44. NO RETALIATION FOR GENUINE SAFETY REPORTS

Users must not threaten, harass, or retaliate against another person solely because that person made a genuine safety report, participated in an investigation, or sought lawful assistance.

This does not prevent a person from responding to an allegation, providing evidence, or exercising legal rights.

Knowingly false allegations remain subject to Section 34.

## 45. FAIRNESS AND CONTEXT

WOOWOO may consider context when applying these Guidelines.

Relevant factors may include:

- severity;
- immediate risk;
- intent where reasonably identifiable;
- repetition;
- impact;
- age;
- power imbalance;
- whether conduct was invited or unwanted;
- whether a warning was ignored; and
- available evidence.

WOOWOO does not guarantee identical consequences for superficially similar incidents where material circumstances differ.

## 46. APPEALS OR REQUESTS FOR REVIEW

Where WOOWOO takes a significant non-emergency access or Membership action, an affected user may contact WOOWOO and request review.

The user should provide relevant information or explain why the decision should be reconsidered.

WOOWOO may maintain an urgent safety restriction while a review is pending where reasonably necessary.

A review request does not prevent either party from exercising legal rights.

## 47. PUBLIC STATEMENTS AND CONFIDENTIALITY

WOOWOO may limit public disclosure of incident details to protect privacy, minors, safety, investigations, or legal obligations.

WOOWOO is not required to publicly disclose confidential evidence or another user's personal information merely because an enforcement action is discussed publicly.

Users remain free to seek lawful advice, report matters to authorities, or exercise statutory rights.

## 48. NO GUARANTEE OF A RISK-FREE ENVIRONMENT

WOOWOO takes community standards and safety seriously, but no physical or digital community can be guaranteed to be entirely free from disagreement, misconduct, accidents, or third-party actions.

These Guidelines establish behavioural expectations and enforcement mechanisms.

They do not constitute a guarantee that no user will ever violate the Guidelines.

Nothing in this Section excludes WOOWOO's responsibility where liability arises under applicable law.

## 49. PRIVACY

Personal data connected with reports, incidents, Memberships, bookings, Events, official community channels, and enforcement may be processed according to the WOOWOO ART HOUSE® Privacy Policy and applicable law.

Information may be restricted to persons who reasonably need it for safety, administration, investigation, legal compliance, or dispute handling.

Users should avoid publicly sharing unnecessary personal information about another person when reporting a concern.

WOOWOO may apply an updated safety rule immediately where reasonably necessary to address a current and material safety risk.
